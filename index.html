Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Timeline of Quaid-e-Azam's Life

  • 1876: Born in Karachi.
  • 1883: Enrolled in Sindh Madrasatul Islam.
  • 1892: Went to England for studies at Lincoln's Inn.
  • 1896: Became a lawyer.
  • 1906: Entered politics.
  • 1913: Joined All India Muslim League.
  • 1920: Left Congress.
  • 1940: Lahore Resolution passed.
  • 1947: Pakistan independence.
  • 1948: Passed away in Karachi.
